Malai Boti

Creamy, tender, and perfectly grilled meat skewers prepared with mild spices, fresh cream, and yogurt marination. A local luxury item designed to melt in your mouth.

prep time

15 min.

cook time

15 min.

servings

4 servings

Tags : BBQ Chicken

instructions

step 1

Marinate the tender chicken chunks with fresh heavy cream, yogurt, white pepper, and clean green chili paste for 4 hours.

step 2

Skewer the marinated chicken chunks carefully onto fine metallic skewers.

step 3

Grill gently over burning hot charcoal embers, brushing with ghee or butter regularly until perfectly tender and smoky.

ingredients

1 kg Chicken Boneless cubes

1 cup Fresh Heavy Cream

1/2 cup Yogurt

tools

Barbeque Skewers

Charcoal Grill Pit

Basting Brush
